# GTA 6 Leaks & Cyberleek Investigation Report

A deep, evidence-first investigation into the August 2026 *Grand Theft Auto VI* leaked footage and map images distributed through the Cyberleek project, the Solana blockchain, and the Arweave permaweb.

> **WARNING**: The gameplay footage itself appears to be **authentic Rockstar Games internal footage** (confirmed by active DMCA takedowns from Rockstar Games and/or Take-Two Interactive)

> **CONTEXT**: This leak surfaced August 17–18, 2026, nine days before the official "GTA VI: An Extended Look" Netflix premiere (August 27, 2026, 3:00 PM ET). GTA VI ships November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

> **SCOPE NOTE**: This repo documents forensic *metadata* — hashes, codec fingerprints, blockchain timestamps, provenance chains analysis.
