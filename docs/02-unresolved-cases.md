# 6. Unresolved Cases & Limitations

- **Raw master footage**: the untouched, non-watermarked source MP4s (pre-FFmpeg `Lavf60.16.100` re-encode) have not been publicly located on any open storage gateway.
- **Exact internal recording timestamp**: HUD, lighting, and audio confirm a post-2024 PS5 development capture, but the precise day/time it was recorded inside Rockstar cannot be confirmed without unedited camera/capture-card EXIF.
- **Map Sneak Peek 1 block data**: the Arweave transaction for `map_sneak_peek_1.png` could not be located via GraphQL owner query. The file is reachable via gateway, but its block timestamp is unconfirmed (Tier 2 evidence).
- **Cyberleek "manifesto"**: the group published three stated commandments (no digital pre-orders, no fake DLC, mandatory offline mode). Presented as activism, but widely viewed by the community as cover for the token promotion — see [`04-scam-forensics.md`](04-scam-forensics.md).
